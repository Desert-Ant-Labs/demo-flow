title: Triage a support inbox
description: Takes the personal data out of support tickets, then drafts a reply or sends each
  ticket to the team that speaks its language.
summary: "Mask personal data, then reply or route each ticket by language."
steps:
  - id: in
    type: textlist
    with:
      items:
        - Hi, I'm Sarah Collins. My card ending 4417 was charged twice for order 88213. Please call
          me at +1 312 555 0187.
        - Hola, soy Javier Morales. No puedo entrar en mi cuenta desde ayer. Mi correo es
          javi.morales@example.com
        - The new dark mode looks great, thanks team!
        - Bonjour, je suis Claire Dubois. Ma livraison n'est jamais arrivée au 12 rue des Lilas à
          Lyon.
        - Could you add an export to PDF? We would pay for it. Tom at Brightline Studio
        - Hallo, hier ist Jonas Becker. Die App stürzt ab, wenn ich Fotos hochlade.
        - My package never arrived and nobody answers my emails. Order 77120, Mike Reyes,
          mike.reyes@example.com
  - id: each
    type: foreach
    uses:
      items: in.items
    with:
      mode: lines
  - id: redact
    type: redact
    uses:
      text: each.item
  - id: tongue
    type: tongue
    uses:
      text: each.item
  - id: if
    type: if
    uses:
      value: redact.text
      test: tongue.language
    with:
      op: equals
      operand: en
  - id: emo
    type: emo
    uses:
      text: if.yes
    with:
      limit: 2
  - id: route
    type: template
    uses:
      a: if.no
      b: tongue.language
    with:
      template: Send to the {{b|lang}} team
  - id: reply
    type: template
    uses:
      a: if.yes
      b: emo.emoji
    with:
      template: Reply {{b}}
  - id: merge
    type: merge
    uses:
      a: reply.text
      b: route.text
  - id: table
    type: table
    uses:
      a: redact.text
      b: tongue.language
      c: merge.value
    with:
      columns: Ticket, Language, Next step
